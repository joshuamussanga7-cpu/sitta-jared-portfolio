## Technical Architecture
I chose **Flutter** for the frontend to ensure a smooth, native experience on both Android and iOS. For the backend, **Firebase** provided the perfect suite of tools:
- **Firestore**: For real-time product listings and user data.
- **Firebase Auth**: Secure role-based access for buyers and sellers.
- **Firebase Storage**: Optimized image hosting for product photos.

### 2. Handling Heavy Media
Marketplaces are image-heavy. To prevent the app from becoming slow, I implemented client-side image compression. Before any photo leaves the student's phone, it's optimized to ensure fast uploads and low data consumption.

## What's Next?
Uni Connect is just the beginning. I'm currently exploring the integration of automated payment gateways and a community-based delivery system to make campus trading even easier.
