# Building Unni Planner (UniMate)

![Unni Planner Home](/projects/Screenshot_20260409_163417_uni planner.jpg)

Being a student is demanding. Between lectures, assignments, and exams, staying organized is a job in itself. I built **Unni Planner** to be the ultimate academic companion that handles the scheduling so students can focus on learning.

## The Problem
Many students rely on manual planners or generic calendar apps that don't understand the specific needs of university life—like recurring weekly timetables, GPA tracking, and assignment deadlines.

![Timetable View](/projects/Screenshot_20260409_163426_uni planner.jpg)

## Key Features Built

### 1. Dynamic Timetable System
The heart of Unni Planner is its flexible timetable. I built a custom scheduling logic that allows students to input their weekly classes with ease. It supports different types of sessions (Lectures, Labs, Seminars) and provides a clean, visual overview of the week.

![Notification and Task Settings](/projects/Screenshot_20260409_163432_uni planner.jpg)

### 2. Smart Notifications
One of the most requested features was reminders. I implemented local notifications that alert students 15–30 minutes before a class or exam starts. The best part? It works completely offline.

### 3. GPA & Performance Tracking
To help students keep their eyes on the goal, I integrated a GPA calculator. It allows users to input their grades and see their semester and cumulative performance instantly.

![GPA and Performance Dashboard](/projects/Screenshot_20260409_163447_uni planner.jpg)

## Technical Highlights
- **State Management**: Used **Provider** to ensure data flows smoothly across the timetable, task list, and GPA sections.
- **Local Persistence**: Integrated local storage to ensure that even without data/Wi-Fi, a student always knows where their next class is.
- **User Experience**: Designed with a focus on "one-handed" use, making it easy to check schedules while walking to class.

## Availability
Unni Planner is currently available on the Google Play Store, helping students stay on top of their academic journey every single day.
